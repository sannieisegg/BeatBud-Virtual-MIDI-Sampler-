🌱🎵 Welcome to Beat Buds 🌱🎵

Music production is for everyone, and I want to contribute in making this a reality. You don't need money to start; just explore the mapped sounds, experiment, and experience the joy of creating music right at your fingertips. 🙂

(Big shoutout to Pixabay and Voicemod for the free sound samples!)

Press the keys below to trigger sounds:

  • A, S, D, F, G - Kick Samples
  • H, J, K, L - Snare Samples
  • Q, W, E, R, T, Y, U, I, O - SFX Samples
